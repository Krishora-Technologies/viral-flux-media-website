# Viral Flux Media — Marketing Skills Stack Guide

A curated roadmap of open-source agent skills from [`marketingskills`](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills) tailored for **Viral Flux Media**. This stack is designed to dominate **SEO** (Search Engine Optimization), **AEO** (Answer Engine Optimization), **GEO** (Generative & Geo-Targeted Engine Optimization), plus commercial growth across video production, Meta ads, and B2B client acquisition.

---

## 1. Core Stack for SEO, AEO & GEO

These skills directly power your search visibility across Google, Bing, Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, and Google AI Overviews.

| Skill | Status | Primary Focus | Why Viral Flux Media Needs It |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **`seo-audit`** | **Installed** | Traditional & Technical SEO | Full site audits, crawlability, indexation health, CWV diagnostics, title tag rewrites, and on-page checklist. |
| **`ai-seo`** | **Installed** | **AEO & GEO (AI Search)** | Optimizes content for AI search engines (Perplexity, SearchGPT, Claude, Google AI Overviews). Focuses on entity extraction, citation-worthy data, and authoritative formatting. |
| **`schema`** | **Installed** | Structured Data & Entity Graphs | Generates advanced JSON-LD (`LocalBusiness`, `Service`, `FAQPage`, `BreadcrumbList`, `VideoObject`). Crucial for Google Knowledge Graph and LLM entity disambiguation. |
| **`programmatic-seo`** | **Installed** | Scaled Geo-Targeting (GEO) | Framework for building high-quality city and niche pages at scale (e.g., Kalyan, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Ulhasnagar) without triggering thin content or doorway penalties. |
| **`site-architecture`** | **Installed** | Hierarchy & Link Equity | Designs logical page hierarchies, topical clusters, and internal linking paths so PageRank flows efficiently from the homepage to subpages. |
| **`directory-submissions`** | **Installed** | Local Citations & Maps | Guides consistent NAP citations across high-authority business directories (Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMart, Bing Places), boosting Google Maps Local Pack rankings. |
| **`link-building`** | **Installed** | Off-Page Authority | Tactics for earning high-quality backlinks, editorial mentions, and digital PR from regional news and marketing publications. |
